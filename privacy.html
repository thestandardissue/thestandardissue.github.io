Standard Issue Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Standard Issue is a 90-day habit and journal app. It was built so that your plan and journal stay yours. In short: we don't collect, see, or store any of your data.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter (your plan, standards, daily check-ins, journal entries and notes) is stored only on your phone. The app has no account system and no server, and it does not send your data anywhere.

The app saves a daily backup file to your phone's Documents/StandardIssue folder. If you have Android's backup turned on (Google backup, or Seedvault on some phones), Android may include the app's data in your own device backup. That backup is handled by Android and your backup provider, not by us.

Things you choose to share

  • Export: when you tap Export, the app opens Android's share menu with a backup file. Where it goes is up to you.
  • Coach: when you ask the coach, the app opens Android's share menu (or copies to your clipboard) with your question and a summary of your plan. If you turned on journal access, the summary also includes your last two weeks of journal entries. Nothing is sent until you pick an app such as Claude or ChatGPT. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Purchases

The Pro upgrade is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Pro. We never receive your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

Analytics, ads and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Children

Standard Issue is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything the app stored. Backup files you exported, or files in Documents/StandardIssue, can be deleted like any other file.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: thestandardissue@proton.me
