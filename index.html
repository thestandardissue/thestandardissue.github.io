Standard Issue

A private 90-day plan to rebuild yourself, one small daily standard at a time.

Rock bottom became the solid foundation on which I rebuilt my life. ~ J.K. Rowling
  • Pick 2 or 3 daily standards. Small things, like "Walked 20 minutes".
  • About 2 minutes a day. Tick them off, then a quick check-in at night.
  • Miss a day? Fine. Just don't miss two in a row. Nothing ever resets.
  • Private by design. No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Your journal stays on your phone.
